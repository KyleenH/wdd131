Can't Judge the Book by it's Cover

This name helps describe the purpose of hiding book covers to help avoid stereotyping.

Purpose:

A website to help readers broaden their range of books without the influence of it's cover. It will show local bookstores that are hosting book clubs for the book selected.

Everyone has heard the saying "Don't judge a book by it's cover". It's good advice, the purpose of a books cover is to help convay the direction of a book at a glance. I have a horrible habit of not wanting to read certain books based off of their covers.

Scenarios:

What kind of genres does this book entail?

What other books has this author written?
