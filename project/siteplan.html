What is this website about?

Everyone knows the saying, "Don't judge a book by it's cover". I myself am very guilty of doing just that. If I don't find a cover aesthetically pleasing I will not look at the summary. Who knows how many great books I have passed up on because the cover didn't interest me. That's why I came up with a website that will provide different books with their summaries while concealing the cover of the book. My hope is to help broaden readers reading regimends.

Under each summary shows genre of the book as well as other works done by the same author!
